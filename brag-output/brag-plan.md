# VoxEye — Hackathon Elevator Pitch & Live Demo Plan (`brag-plan.md`)

- **Spec:** `launch` (Hackathon Elevator Pitch + Live Demo) · `product-native` (3-Zone Stage + Keynote Framing + Real Camera Demo Slot) · `60s` · `16:9` (`1920×1080`, `30fps`) · `default` (warm, upbeat, direct engineering pitch) · `lively` energy · Real VoxEye TTS (`speak.py` / `fal.py`) + Stereo Geiger Sonar (`spatial_sonar.js`) + Upbeat Music (`happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10`, auto-ducked) · References: Hackathon Kickoff & Quintessential Judge Deck (`context/hackathon/kickoff-deck.md`).

## Angle & Hook
- **One-Sentence Angle:** VoxEye turns any smartphone and stereo earphones into a real-time 3D depth sonar, bilingual (English & Greek) voice assistant, and compass tour guide for blind and low-vision people—with zero extra hardware.
- **The Hook (`0.0s – 6.8s`):** Start directly with the person and the 3-way mobility gap ("The 3 Ws / The Person" from the judges' rubric): white canes miss chest/head obstacles, cloud AI lags 2–4 seconds (you collide before it speaks), and dedicated depth hardware costs €5,000+.

## Judges' Rubric Mapping (Quintessential & Track Judges)
1. **Beat 1 — The Person & The Pain (`0.0s – 6.8s`):** A blind traveler navigating everyday streets and indoor spaces; why canes, slow cloud VLMs, and €5,000 rigs leave them stuck—and how a phone + earphones already in their pocket solves it.
2. **Beat 2 — The Solution, Live Product & "Show the Work" (`6.8s – 53.5s`):**
   - **System 1 (Fast Edge Stereo Depth Sonar):** `xr_depth.js` (WebXR ARCore) + `depth_worker.js` / `local_depth.py` (`Depth-Anything-V2-Small` WebGPU/ONNX) + `center_probe.py` metric calibration -> `spatial_sonar.js` 3-sector (`LEFT` / `CENTER` / `RIGHT`) per-ear Geiger beeps (`~5 Hz` always-on, full-speed on Hold-1s).
   - **System 2 (Bilingual EN/EL Voice, Instant Barge-In & Explicit Memory):** `router.py` (`decide_user_command`) + `info_pipeline.py` + `memory.py` (`keep_memory=True` only for explicit goals/notes) + `/panel` (`trace.html`) live judge telemetry ("Show the work").
   - **Tour Guide & Hackathon Sponsor/Track Stack:** Phone GPS + tilt-compensated compass (`looming.js` + `service.py`) + **Google AI** (`Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite / 3.8 Flash / 3.8 Live`, `Google Maps/Places/Routes API`) + **fal.ai** (`fal-ai/elevenlabs/tts/eleven-v3` 24kHz PCM streaming) + **Open Track Hardware Bonus & Voice Agents Track**.
   - **Real-World Camera Demo Showcase (`38.2s – 53.5s`):** Dedicated 15.3s viewport showcasing real camera footage of a user walking and interacting with VoxEye (`assets/real-demo.mp4` slot, with synchronized HUD & telemetry).
3. **Beat 3 — What's Next (`53.5s – 60.0s`):** Next week's roadmap (vertical overhang/curb bands, 3D HRTF Soundscape beacons, offline neural TTS) + live URL / repo CTA.
