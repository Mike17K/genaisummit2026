"The model answering is the boring part. The model acting is the new part."

### 13 — Your toolbox today: six things that are a weekend now
1. **Agents that use tools** — call APIs, run code, browse, click through real apps, keep going for hours.
2. **Eyes and ears** — photos, screenshots, PDFs, video, live audio as input.
3. **Real-time voice** — interruptions, tone, almost no lag.
4. **Huge context** — a whole codebase, contract binder or year of tickets in one prompt.
5. **Software on demand** — working apps from a sentence or sketch; UI generated per user, per moment.
6. **Connectors everywhere** — MCP and friends: mail, calendars, docs, databases.

### 14 — Pick your altitude: the ladder
Prompt → + Context (your data in the window) → + Tools (it can do things) → Agent (it decides what to do next) → Many agents (a team with roles). "Use the lowest rung that solves the problem. Then climb one higher for the demo."

### 15 — Section 03: Ideas to steal

### 16 — This weekend's theme
"Agents are moving out of the demo and into the job." So the question for every idea: what is the tedious, multi-step job nobody wants to do twice, and can an agent do all of it end to end?

### 17 — Direction 1 · the work nobody wants: Agents at the office
- **The phone call you avoid** — voice agent that waits on hold, negotiates, reports back what was agreed.
- **The meeting that writes its own tickets** — listens in, creates tasks, assigns owners, chases them a week later.
- **Inbox to done** — reads mail, drafts replies, books the meeting, files the invoice; shows a diff of your day.
- **On-call sidekick** — alert fires at 3am; reads logs, finds the commit, proposes the fix.

### 18 — Direction 2 · science, faster: Agents in the lab
- **The lab notebook that listens** — talk at the bench; get a structured protocol, results table and tomorrow's reagent order.
- **Fifty papers before lunch** — reads the literature, proposes the next experiment, cites the page for each claim.
- **Trial matcher** — patient summary in; clinical trials they could join out, in plain language.
- **Instrument whisperer** — watches raw lab-machine output, flags odd runs, drafts the report.

### 19 — Direction 3 · content and digital art: Agents in the studio
- **Script to storyboard** — a script page in; consistent characters, shots and rough animatic out.
- **The game that builds itself** — levels, rules and art generated from how you played the last round.
- **Campaign in a box** — one brief; every format and channel, checked against the brand guide by an agent that says no.
- **Visuals that listen** — live generative visuals driven by music, the room or the speaker's voice.

### 20 — Direction 4 · the physical world (hardware earns extra points): Agents in the wild
- **Grandma's button** — one physical button; a voice reads her letters, explains the bill, calls the pharmacy.
- **Point and ask** — aim a camera at a machine, shelf or monument; get answers.
- **Bureaucracy navigator** — "I need to renew X." Finds the forms, fills what it can, says what to bring.
- **Argue with Socrates** — pitch your startup; he only answers with questions.

### 23 — What you're judged on: Usefulness, craft, and aura points
1. **Usefulness** — would a real person use this on Tuesday? Show who they are and the job the agent takes off their hands.
2. **Craft** — runs live, survives the ugly case, details feel considered, not just polished.
3. **Aura points** — the thing we're still talking about on the way home. Taste, nerve, a surprise.

### 24 — Scope

### 25 — Pace · 29 hours from first commit to code freeze
- **Sat 13:00 Decide** — team, track, the person it's for. Kill other ideas before lunch.
- **Sat → 20:00 Ugly end-to-end** — input to output working by dinner, however badly.

- **Sun morning** — polish the path (only what the demo touches). Record the video early.
- **Sun 18:00 Code freeze** — repo, demo video, project details submitted, not "almost".
- **Sun 19:00 · Mon 18:00 Pitch** — 8 semifinalists, then 4 finalists on the Summit stage at SNFCC.

### 26 — Field rules
- **Demo first** — write the 3-minute script before the code; build only what it needs.
- **Use real data** — real inbox, PDF, photo; lorem ipsum hides every interesting problem.
- **Let the agents build it** — coding agents for scaffolding; save your brain for the idea.
- **Show the work** — stream steps, tool calls, sources; watching an agent work is half the magic.
- **Have a plan B** — Wi-fi dies, APIs rate-limit. Cache a good run; the demo video is insurance.
- **Ask us** — stuck for 20 minutes? Find Socrates or Kostas.

### 27 — The pitch (same format Sunday night and on the Summit stage): three minutes, three beats
- **0:00 The person** — who has this job and why it hurts. One sentence, no market size.
- **0:20 The live demo** — straight to the wow moment; let the agent run; stop talking while it works.
- **2:30 What's next** — what you'd build with one more week.
