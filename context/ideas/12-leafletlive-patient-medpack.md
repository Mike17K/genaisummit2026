## End-to-End Job & Live Wow Moment
1. **Input (`0:20` Wow Moment):** Snap a photo of a real Greek medication box on stage (or drop an official EMA SmPC PDF) + a patient's current medication list.
2. **Extraction & Interaction Check (`Google Gemini` + `treg.to`):** Extracts exact dosing rules, storage conditions, and drug-drug interactions with verbatim section citations.
3. **Clinical Safety Gate (`Oumi Iris`):** Scores every instruction before showing it to the patient (`contraindication_detected`, `dosage_hallucination_risk`, `requires_physician_escalation`).
4. **Visual & Voice Care Companion (`fal.ai` + `Voice`):** Generates a crystal-clear step-by-step visual administration storyboard via `fal` (e.g., inhaler/injection/timing visual cards), schedules reminders in Google Calendar (`boxd run googlecalendar`), and answers patient questions by voice in Greek or English.
