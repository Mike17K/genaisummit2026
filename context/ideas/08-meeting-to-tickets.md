# 8. The meeting that writes its own tickets

**Direction:** Agents at the office. **Risk:** Medium.

- **Job:** listens to a live meeting, creates tasks, assigns owners, then chases them a week later.
- **Wow moment:** speak a decision on stage and the ticket appears in Linear or Jira; fast-forward a week and it sends the nudge.
- **Why it scores:** shows the proactive, "keeps going" behaviour the brief asks for.
- **Risks:** live audio transcription and noisy rooms; simulate the week-later chase with a clock override.

## Judge angle
- **Track:** Voice & Enterprise (Wonderful)
- **Twist to make it memorable:** Run the demo as a mock judging meeting; the agent assigns the judges follow-ups, then the 'week later' chase fires live with a clock override.
- **Production proof to show:** Owner-confirmation step, dedupes existing tickets, trace per ticket back to the transcript line.
