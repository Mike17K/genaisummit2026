# 21. Agent-run micro-business

**Direction:** Agents first (agents are the user or operator). **Risk:** High. **Track:** Content & Digital Art / Open.

- **Who the user is:** A solo maker who wants a product line without the marketing and ops work.
- **Job:** Give the agents EUR 20 and a goal; they design a product (fal), write the listing, find buyers (treg), and report; the human approves any spend.
- **Wow moment:** A storefront page and first outreach appear from one sentence, with the budget tracker ticking down.
- **Sponsor stack:** fal for product images and video, treg for buyer discovery, Google credits for reasoning.
- **Risks:** Real money and outreach are risky; keep to sandbox and approved drafts.

## Judge angle
- **Twist:** A funny but real end-to-end loop that makes judges ask 'could that actually work?'
- **Production proof to show:** Spend cap, human approval for every external action, full action log.
