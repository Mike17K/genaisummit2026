# 10. Campaign in a box, with a brand guardian

**Direction:** Agents in the studio. **Risk:** Medium.

- **Job:** one brief in; every format and channel out (posts, email, banner, short video script), each checked by a separate agent against the brand guide that is allowed to say no.
- **Wow moment:** the guardian rejects a draft on screen, the creator agent revises, and it passes.
- **Why it scores:** many agents with roles, visible disagreement, and a clear paying user (small marketing team).
- **Risks:** output quality of images; keep the focus on the loop, not the art.

## Judge angle
- **Track:** Content & Digital Art (fal)
- **Twist to make it memorable:** Show the brand guardian rejecting the creator agent, with reasons, in a visible argument. Use fal for images and a short video; the guardian blocks one off-brand frame.
- **Production proof to show:** Cost per asset, brand guide as versioned rules, human sign-off, output checked before publish.
