# 6. Grandma's button

**Direction:** Agents in the wild (hardware extra points). **Risk:** High.

- **Job:** one physical button reads her letters aloud, explains the bill and calls the pharmacy.
- **Why it scores:** emotional and memorable; hardware earns extra points per the deck.
- **Risks:** needs hardware, camera and voice; large scope for 36 hours.

## Judge angle
- **Track:** Open (hardware extra points)
- **Twist to make it memorable:** Bring the real button. A volunteer 'grandma' presses it on stage with a real letter; the agent reads it, explains it and makes the call.
- **Production proof to show:** Works offline-ish with cached run, privacy by design (nothing stored), one-button failure fallback.
