# 13. Freelancer paperwork autopilot

**Direction:** Agents at the office. **Risk:** Medium.

- **Job:** collects receipts and invoices from email and photos, categorises them, chases unpaid clients, and prepares the quarterly summary for the accountant.
- **Wow moment:** photograph a pile of receipts and the ledger and a polite chase email appear.
- **Why it scores:** tedious, repeated, and every judge knows someone who hates it.
- **Risks:** tax rules vary; keep it to prep and summary, not filing.

## Judge angle
- **Track:** Voice & Enterprise / Open
- **Twist to make it memorable:** Photograph receipts live; it sorts them, catches a duplicate and an overdue invoice, and sends the chase in the freelancer's tone.
- **Production proof to show:** Human approval before anything is sent, reconciliation check, data stays local.
