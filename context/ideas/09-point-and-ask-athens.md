# 9. Point and ask: Athens

**Direction:** Agents in the wild. **Risk:** Medium.

- **Job:** aim a phone camera at a monument, shelf, menu or machine and get answers; agent also plans the route and books or checks opening times.
- **Wow moment:** point at the Parthenon or a street sign and it explains, then plans the next stop.
- **Why it scores:** local flavour and easy for judges to try in the room.
- **Risks:** looks like a plain chatbot unless the agent takes real actions (maps, tickets, bookings).

## Judge angle
- **Track:** Open (hardware: phone/glasses)
- **Twist to make it memorable:** Add the Socrates twist from the deck: it answers only with questions until you ask for the answer. Chain real actions: tickets, route, opening hours.
- **Production proof to show:** Tool-call trace on screen, graceful failure when the camera sees something unknown.
