> I’m Manos Koukoumidis, co-founder and CEO of Oumi. Before starting Oumi, I led generative AI at Google Cloud, including developing Google Cloud PaLM, which eventually became Gemini. We built Oumi because we believe companies should own the AI they depend on, rather than simply rent it.

> This weekend, every participant at the summit gets free early access to Iris, our state-of-the-art decision model. Decision models have been receiving very significant attention across the industry in the last two months.
> 
> As a decision model, Iris is a different from common LLMs. It doesn’t generate text. Instead, you give it a situation and one or more questions, and it returns a probability for every possible answer, all in a single call. Those questions can be yes/no, multiple choice, or ratings on a scale.
> 
> That makes Iris useful as the decision-making layer inside a larger AI system: your LLM, voice model, or image model creates; Iris decides what should happen next.
> 
> ### How to get access
> Register at https://labs.oumi.ai/hackathon-village/genai-summit-athens-2026. Each teammate should register individually. We’ll email you an API key and quick-start guide before Saturday.

> Make your first call using our guide: https://docs.oumi.ai/guides/decision-models
> 
> ### Some ideas for each track
> 
> #### Voice agents
> * **Know when to escalate:** On every turn, Iris can estimate whether a caller is angry, confused, or ready to buy, helping your agent hand off to a person at the right moment.
> * **Route intelligently:** Have Iris decide whether a request belongs with booking, billing, another workflow, or a human.
> 
> #### Healthcare / AI biotech
> * **Triage:** Have Iris rate the urgency of a patient message, from routine self-care to emergency, before your chatbot responds.
> * **Safety checks:** Ask Iris whether a proposed response crosses a boundary, such as giving a diagnosis when it shouldn’t, and block or escalate it when necessary.
> 
> #### Creative generative AI
> * **Creative director:** Generate 20 takes, have Iris score them against your creative brief, and keep the best three.
> * **Interactive storytelling:** In a video, game, or audio experience, use Iris to decide what happens next based on what the audience just did. *(Iris supports image and text, so video will require frame extraction libraries and audio will require speech to text)*
> 
> #### Open category / games
> * **NPCs that think:** Each character can use Iris to decide whether to fight, flee, trade, cooperate, or lie based on the current situation.
> * **Play the game itself:** Give Iris the current game state and the actions available to a player, then let it choose what to do next. Use it to power an AI opponent, teammate, or fully autonomous player that makes decisions turn by turn.
