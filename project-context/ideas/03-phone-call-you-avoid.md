# 3. The phone call you avoid

**Direction:** Agents at the office. **Risk:** High. **Status:** biggest aura potential.

- **Job:** a voice agent calls a business, waits on hold, negotiates and reports back what was agreed.
- **Wow moment:** play the live call on stage.
- **Why it scores:** memorable demo, strong aura points.
- **Risks:** telephony and voice latency are hard in 36 hours; cache a good run and record the demo video early.

## Judge angle
- **Track:** Voice & Enterprise (Wonderful)
- **Twist to make it memorable:** Two agents on stage: yours calls a scripted 'customer service' agent that stonewalls it; yours negotiates live. Works in Greek.
- **Production proof to show:** Hard spending cap and stop word, call transcript with decisions highlighted, plan B recording.
