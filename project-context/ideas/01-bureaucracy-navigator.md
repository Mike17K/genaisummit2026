# 1. Bureaucracy navigator (Greece)

**Direction:** Agents in the wild / at the office. **Risk:** Low. **Status:** top pick.

- **User:** someone who must renew a document, register a business or move address and dislikes gov.gr forms.
- **Job:** type "I need to renew X". The agent finds the right forms, fills what it can from an uploaded ID or PDF, and says what to bring.
- **Wow moment:** the agent drives a real form live, with steps on screen, and stops at "bring this stamped paper".
- **Why it scores:** pain is local and obvious to Athens judges; real data (PDF/ID) is easy to show.
- **Risks:** real government sites may change or block automation; keep a cached run as plan B.

## Judge angle
- **Track:** Open (or Voice & Enterprise)
- **Twist to make it memorable:** It refuses to guess: when a form field is ambiguous the agent asks one sharp question, and ends with a printable 'what to bring' checklist. Do it in Greek and English.
- **Production proof to show:** Approval step before submitting, audit log of every field it filled and why, recovery when the site layout differs.
