# 7. Lease reviewer and negotiator

**Direction:** Agents at the office. **Risk:** Low.

- **User:** a renter (or small landlord) handed a 20-page lease in Greek or English.
- **Job:** read the real PDF, flag risky clauses with the page they came from, compare to typical terms, draft the negotiation email and a checklist of questions.
- **Wow moment:** drop in a real lease; clauses highlight live with citations and a ready-to-send reply appears.
- **Why it scores:** real data, real person, "Tuesday" test passes easily; huge-context toolbox item.
- **Risks:** not legal advice, so add a disclaimer; legal accuracy needs a sources-shown approach.

## Judge angle
- **Track:** Open or Voice & Enterprise
- **Twist to make it memorable:** Judges bring their own anonymised contract: it finds the sneaky clause live. Show a 'what changes if you sign' diff against the standard terms.
- **Production proof to show:** Every flag links to the page and quote, no legal-advice claims, runs on a real PDF with scans.
