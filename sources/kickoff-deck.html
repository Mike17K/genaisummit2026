The rules

01

Teams of 1–5

No team yet? Team formation starts right after this.

02

Build it here

Bring ideas, tools and boilerplate. Projects substantially built before the start may be disqualified.

03

Use what helps

Open-source code, public APIs and partner tools are fine. Follow their licences and credit what you didn't build.

04

You own it

You own what you build. We may show your project, demo, video and team names on the site and in event and promotional material.

05

Respect the venue

No alcohol: our hosts' rule, and this is a research facility. No harassment or abuse of any kind.

06

Cameras around

The weekend is photographed and filmed. Rather not appear? Tell a member of the team.
