# Agenda · Gen AI Summit Hackathon

All times Athens (EET). Only confirmed times are listed; anything not here is not set yet.

## Judge notes

- Some of you are joining us on Sunday, some on Monday, and some for both. Please read the details relevant to the days you're attending. All times are Athens time.
- Sunday, 15 November: please arrive at Athens LifeTech Park a little before 18:00 so we can get everyone settled and walk you through everything. All teams submit at 18:00. After shortlisting, 8 semifinalists pitch around 19:00, with 3 minutes per team. You'll then choose the 4 finalists for Monday. We're aiming to announce them around 20:00–20:30.
- Monday, 16 November: please arrive at Book Castle, SNFCC, a little before 18:00. The 4 finalists pitch from 18:00–18:30, with 3 minutes per team. You'll then decide the final rankings, and we'll announce the winners at 18:50.
- We'll give you access to a simple voting web app on the day and walk you through it. We'll also run an AI-assisted review of the submissions and pass you notes to help with your assessment.
- We'd love you to use your own experience and expertise and look at the whole picture: what the team has shipped, how well it works, and the potential you see in both the team and what they've built. Focus on work completed during the hackathon.
- Watch the demos, ask questions, and help choose the finalists on Sunday and the winners on Monday. Your honest, practical feedback will mean a lot to the teams.
- If you haven't already, please confirm which day or days you're coming by replying to your invitation email or accepting the calendar invitations.
- Sunday venue: Athens LifeTech Park, Block 49, 31st Street, Foinikas, 190 04 Spata ([map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Athens+LifeTech+Park+Spata)).
