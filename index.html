Navigating Blind Shouldn’t Cost €4,000 or Lag 3 Seconds

285 million people live with visual impairment (43 million blind worldwide). Yet existing solutions trap pedestrians in a dangerous hardware-versus-latency gap.

White Canes Miss What Hangs Above

A white cane only sweeps ground-to-waist (0.8–1.0m). Overhanging tree branches, street signs, open cabinets, and truck mirrors sit right at chest and forehead height — causing over 80% of upper-body collisions.

dual-process engineering

How VoxEye Works: System 1 Reflexes + System 2 Brain

Academic SOTA (PathFinder, WalkVLM, DualVLN) proves blind navigation requires two parallel loops: an instant non-verbal depth reflex and a deliberative bilingual voice router.

1

System 1 · Always-On Stereo Depth Sonar

Runs Depth-Anything-V2-Small (phone WebGPU / server ONNX) + WebXR ARCore depth (xr_depth.js), re-anchored to metric meters via center_probe.py.

Android Stereo Breakthrough: Opening a Bluetooth headset mic on Android pins audio to mono 8kHz SCO call mode. VoxEye explicitly opens the phone’s built-in mic on push-to-talk and recreates the sonar AudioContext so earphones stay in crystal-clear stereo!

spatial_sonar.js · depth_view.js ~5 Hz Always-On
2

System 2 · Bilingual Voice, Barge-In & Memory

Every English or Greek (Ελληνικά) utterance passes through router.py:decide_user_command(). Routine questions never pollute memory (keep_memory=False), while explicit goals/notes (keep_memory=True) persist across turns.

Instant Barge-In & Guaranteed Reply: Pressing the button or speaking cuts any ongoing speech in <50ms, and clause-streamed TTS (speak.py / fal.py ElevenLabs v3) speaks within ~1s.

router.py · info_pipeline.py EN & Ελληνικά
3

Compass Tour Guide & Live Judges’ Trace

Fuses phone GPS + tilt-compensated magnetometer (looming.js) with Google Places, Geocoding & Routes API (plus OpenStreetMap fallback) to guide users in exact steps and clock-face directions relative to where their body faces.

Observable Craft (/panel): Streams every heard utterance, router decision, tool call, memory state, and audio diagnostic live for hackathon judges.
